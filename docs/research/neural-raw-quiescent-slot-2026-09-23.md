# Temporary handoff of an idle inference slot

This additive scheduling amendment may stop only the registered raw-inference parent PID 107405, while wrapper PID 107249 remains alive and waiting. It does not change any numerical source, task, feature, checkpoint, selection, raw-score archive, or evaluation rule.

Before stopping, the helper binds the original queue plan and source, verifies the first three complete stages (16 proxy AV tasks, 15 original nonstudent tasks, and 48 randomized non-AV tasks) and their current all-42-recording/all-four-checkpoint numerical and reuse gates. The final proxy stage must have no launch receipt and still lack at least one of its frozen readiness prerequisites. Exact boot ID, start ticks, command, and PID identify both owned processes.

The helper sends SIGSTOP only to the owned parent, then requires state T and an empty complete descendant tree. This check after stopping closes the child-launch race. It records the CUDA process query, but does not assume WSL exposes CUDA PIDs. A known parent CUDA allocation fails the handoff. If any verification after the stop fails, a failure receipt prohibits a student grant and requires explicit root recovery; the helper never silently resumes or terminates another process.

A successful proof states that the predecessor remains alive and paused. It is not an exit receipt. A new streaming student worker must bind the proof and revalidate the live stopped identity and empty descendants before its initial launch. Once accepted, subsequent student tasks bind that initial handoff; they do not require the predecessor to remain stopped indefinitely.

The raw parent can resume only after the owned nonstudent fit parent PID 97282 exits with observed exit code zero and all 72 registered randomized/proxy non-AV fit numerical and applicable reuse gates pass. The helper revalidates the paused identity immediately before SIGCONT and records the resumed state and timestamp. Two student workers plus the resumed raw worker retain the maximum of three active GPU workers. Later precision inference continues to require the original completed raw queue, wrapper exit, and full prerequisite gates.

Every plan, pause/failure, validation, and resume receipt is a new immutable NAS file. No action is authorized by mere passage of time. This document and helper must be bound before execution. All CPU work uses the already granted scheduling slot and NAS runtime paths; no GPU computation or metric evaluation runs in this helper.
