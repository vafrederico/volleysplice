# Shorter temporal context: prospective development protocol

Chosen after the complete, audited short-rally boost comparison on 2026-09-19. No results from this context intervention have been generated or examined. This is an adaptive development experiment on previously inspected groups, not a protected-test confirmation.

The hypothesis is that the existing temporal network can miss brief action when its temporal support is too broad. Reducing its receptive field may improve rally retention without changing its parameter count, visual representation or supervision. The intervention tests temporal support only; existing audiovisual feature windows and whole-record preprocessing remain unchanged.

Use the `reviewed_export` cohort and `short_boost` loss arm in both compact audiovisual TCN and frozen-DINO+TCN. This is the only cohort where the preceding DINO boost passed the full recovery screen against both baseline and matched global-positive control. Its compact counterpart still failed the recovery screen. Holding that cohort and loss fixed tests the remaining architecture difference while preserving the successful DINO training setup.

The original-context reference scores at the fixed product padding are compact F1_padP_coreR 0.9030769863993592 / R_core 0.9240923129240207 and DINO F1_padP_coreR 0.9251267295840396 / R_core 0.9695454260469211. Mean completely missed rallies are 33 and 13.6667 respectively; mean short-rally complete losses are 21.6667 and 11. Their different loss floors prevent interpreting raw recovery counts as directly comparable effects. The DINO boost improved F1 in all three seeds but only two of four source groups versus each control. None of the preceding standard F1 screens or cross-architecture transfer screens passed.

Change only the five temporal-block dilations from `(1,2,4,8,16)` to `(1,1,2,2,2)`, with kernel size 5. Receptive field changes from 125 to 33 ticks at 4 Hz; the extreme sample centers are approximately 31 and 8 seconds apart. Keep the same 64 hidden channels, residual blocks, initialization, dropout, four heads, input features and all tensor shapes. Trainable parameter counts remain 29,700 compact and 46,868 downstream DINO, excluding the frozen encoder. The context profile must be bound externally because a state dictionary alone does not encode dilations.

Preserve the original 128 central training ticks plus 62 ticks of halo on each side, including sampling, valid-segment resets and dropout streams. This controls the context comparison rather than changing the training chunk schedule. Keep all head weights, short-positive multipliers, original-duration short membership, exact-only scaler/class-weight fitting, group exclusion, optimizer and checkpoint schedule unchanged. The keep head remains auxiliary during decoding.

Use the same repaired audiovisual and DINO manifests, all 18 available nonbeach recordings and their exact/draft/reviewed-export supervision. Evaluation remains the same eight exact-label recordings, 322 rallies and four source groups. Beach and protected source group `source-group-008` remain excluded. Do not read its protected labels or predictions. Gold, ignored intervals and source grouping are unchanged.

Run seeds 3407, 1729 and 20260918 for both architectures. Reuse six immutable original-context result cells from the completed reference; train six short-context cells. Each new cell has six physical fits for unordered pairs of excluded groups, providing twelve restricted logical inner-validation views, plus four separate outer refits. Both excluded groups stay absent from every supervision tier. An inner view exposes only its designated validation group's predictions. The planned total is 60 new physical fits representing 96 logical fits, with 168 checkpoint pairs / 336 NPZ files. Do not select across seeds.

Qualify physical-fit sharing before registration with the prepared actual-data preflight: fixed seed 3407 and the first two canonical source groups; original-profile epoch-five checkpoint/prediction/history replay against both historical exclusion orders; short-profile union/single/repeated validation fits with equal trained state, exposure history and shared predictions; immutable resume and saved-checkpoint replay. Failure blocks this implementation until diagnosed. No outcome-driven changes to the selected cohort, arm, dilation profile or thresholds are allowed within this run.

Choose epoch from 5/15/30/60 and live/boundary decoding using the unchanged inner grid and selection order. Eligible settings require pooled inner R_core at least 0.95, then maximize F1_padP_coreR; record infeasible fallbacks. Each outer group remains excluded from fitting and inner selection. Checkpoint and decoder settings may be selected independently under that identical rule, so the contrast measures the complete training-and-selection procedure.

The target product padding is fixed at two seconds before and after. Pad, clip and merge model and human ranges identically; join positive gaps strictly below three seconds, retaining the joined time. Exactly three seconds remains a cut. Subtract ignored ranges after joining from model, core human and padded human unions, and never rejoin across an ignored span. Pool duration numerators and denominators across recordings within each seed before computing P_pad, R_core and F1_padP_coreR. Average those seed-level pooled metrics only afterward. Always report all four symmetric padding cases 0/1/2/3 seconds, including model and human export duration and their difference. Rank only the declared two-second case.

Compare short context against the original context within each architecture, using the same cohort, loss arm and seeds. Preserve the preceding recovery screen: mean F1, total core recall and >3-second core recall regressions no worse than 0.005; mean event-F1 regression no worse than 0.01; mean overall and <=3-second complete losses each reduced at least 20%; overall and short incomplete losses do not increase; overall and short complete losses each decrease in at least two seeds. Preserve the separate standard F1 screen: mean gain at least 0.02, at least two positive seeds, a majority of positive source groups, and mean core-recall regression no worse than 0.005. Report every condition, loss identities and per-seed/group effects, including failures.

Declare recovery replication only if both architectures pass the recovery screen with all candidate inner selections feasible. Declare standard-F1 replication separately under the analogous paired rule. Difference-in-differences is descriptive and need not be positive for equal benefit to replicate. Do not substitute raw cross-architecture scores, each model's best padding or a new criterion for the registered rules. Three seeds and four development groups do not support a population confidence claim.

Freeze the prospective protocol, 19 analysis sources, environment, preflight and input/reference identities before candidate fitting. Execute with one worker on the RTX 3080; no concurrent training workers. Require the independent context tensor/scaler/exposure audit and full decoder-selection/interval summary before interpretation. Preserve all unsuccessful engineering checks as separate evidence. No production promotion or phone/browser latency claim follows from this study.
