VolleyCut

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 18, 2026

Short version: VolleyCut processes the videos you choose on your device. The Android app has no internet permission, and the developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your videos or personal data.

Who provides VolleyCut

VolleyCut is provided by Vinicius Frederico. Privacy questions can be sent to vafrederico@gmail.com.

Information the app accesses

VolleyCut accesses a video only after you select it with Android's system file picker. To provide rally detection, editing, and export, the app may read the selected video's image frames, audio track, filename, file size, modification time, duration, dimensions, rotation, and codec information.

How information is used

The selected video and its audio are processed locally to suggest volleyball rally ranges, preview edits, and create exports. VolleyCut stores local project metadata, derived audiovisual features, model results, edit decisions, ignored sections, preferences, and cache files so that projects can be resumed and repeated work can be avoided.

Collection and sharing

The Android app does not transmit user data to the developer or to third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account, or cloud-storage SDK. It does not sell personal information.

When you explicitly export an MP4, edit list, or model-feedback file, Android asks you where to save it. That user-directed export is the only transfer initiated by the app. You control the destination and any later sharing.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used to show progress for user-started analysis and export.
  • Foreground media processing and wake lock: used to keep long, user-started video analysis and export running visibly.
  • Selected-file access: granted by Android only for documents you choose through the system picker.

VolleyCut does not request internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, advertising ID, or broad photo/video-library access.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local project data and feature caches remain on your device until you delete the project or cache, clear VolleyCut's app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Incomplete exports and temporary export files are removed after failure or cancellation where Android's selected storage provider permits it.

Depending on your Android device and backup settings, Android may back up or transfer some app-private preferences or project metadata as part of operating-system backup or device migration. This is controlled by Android and your device account; the VolleyCut developer does not receive that data.

Security

VolleyCut relies on Android app sandboxing and the system document picker. Because the app has no internet permission, its normal media-processing workflow cannot upload your selected recording. No software can guarantee absolute security, so keep your device updated and use trusted storage providers.

Children

VolleyCut is a general-purpose sports video editor and is not directed specifically to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If VolleyCut's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the changed behavior is released. The effective date above identifies the current version.
